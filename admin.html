How to Use This Panel
Read before editing

Step 1: Click any section in the sidebar (Hero, Services, etc.)

Step 2: Edit the text fields — every field corresponds directly to your website.

Step 3: Click "Save & Apply" button — this downloads an updated index.html file.

Step 4: Replace the old index.html in your website folder with the downloaded one.
