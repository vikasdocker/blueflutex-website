Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and sends no data to any advertising or tracking network. If you use the contact form, we store what you typed so we can reply to you. That is the whole of it.

What we collect

Only if you submit the contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The budget range you select, if you select one
  • Your message
  • A salted, truncated hash of your IP address — used only to rate-limit spam, never to identify you
  • Your browser user agent and the page you submitted from

We do not store your raw IP address, and the hash is salted with a value held in our server configuration, so it cannot be reversed or matched against another site’s data.

Why we collect it

Solely to answer your enquiry. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell, rent or share the details with anyone else. If you would like your details deleted, email us and we will remove them.

Fonts are served from our own server

The typefaces on this site are hosted here, not requested from Google. Had we used a third-party font service, every page view would have disclosed your IP address to that provider. Self-hosting avoids that entirely.

Third-party code

The 3D animation in the hero loads three.js from jsDelivr. If your browser downloads it, jsDelivr necessarily sees your IP address, as any content delivery network must. No data is shared with them beyond the ordinary mechanics of serving a file.

If you would rather we did not depend on an external network at all, say so and we will self-host the library instead.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to run the conversation and meet any legal obligation. Rate-limiting records are deleted after 24 hours.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email vikasshu7@gmail.com and we will action it. This site is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Hosting

The site is hosted on free shared infrastructure operated by InfinityFree. As with any web host, that provider necessarily processes the technical information required to deliver the page.
