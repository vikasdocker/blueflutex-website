Summary: BlueFluteX is committed to protecting your privacy. We only collect data necessary to deliver and improve our services, never sell your personal information to third parties, and give you control over your own data. This policy applies to all BlueFluteX products, websites, and services.

01 Information We Collect

We collect information in different ways depending on how you interact with BlueFluteX — whether you visit our website, use one of our applications, or engage our consulting services.

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact & Inquiry Data: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and project details submitted via our website contact form or email.
  • Account Registration: For applications we develop that require accounts — username, email address, and encrypted password.
  • Project & Contract Information: Business details, requirements, and communication records shared during client engagements.
  • Support Requests: Information you provide when seeking technical assistance for any of our products.
  • Payment Information: Billing name, address, and payment card details (processed securely through PCI-DSS compliant payment processors — we do not store raw card numbers).

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, session duration, and interaction patterns within our applications.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, browser type, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Log Data: IP address, access timestamps, referring URLs, and error logs.
  • Location Data: Approximate location derived from IP address, or precise location only when explicitly requested by specific app features and you grant permission.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Analytics providers (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase) that report aggregated usage statistics.
  • App store platforms (Google Play, Apple App Store) that share anonymised download and crash metrics.
  • Cloud platforms (AWS) that provide infrastructure-level logs.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Building, deploying, and maintaining the software or services you have commissioned.
  • Communication: Responding to enquiries, sending project updates, and providing customer support.
  • Product Improvement: Analysing usage patterns to fix bugs, enhance performance, and design new features.
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting obligations under applicable laws including Indian IT law, GDPR (for EU users), and data protection regulations.
  • Security: Detecting fraud, preventing unauthorised access, and protecting user accounts.
  • Billing: Processing payments, issuing invoices, and maintaining financial records as required by law.
  • Marketing (with consent): Sending newsletters or product announcements only when you have explicitly opted in. You may unsubscribe at any time.
We never sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties for commercial purposes.

03 Our Services & Data Practices

Each service we offer has specific data handling characteristics. Below is a breakdown of data practices by service type:

📱 Android Applications

Apps may request permissions for camera, storage, location, or notifications — only when necessary for core functionality. All permissions are declared in the app manifest. You may revoke permissions at any time through Android Settings. Data synced to our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.

🍎 iOS Applications

iOS apps follow Apple's App Store privacy guidelines. We declare all data collection in App Privacy Labels. Location, contacts, and health data are accessed only with explicit user consent via iOS permission dialogs. We comply with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework for any cross-app tracking.

🖥 Desktop Software (PC/Mac/Linux)

Desktop applications may collect crash reports and usage analytics to improve stability. These are anonymised before transmission. License validation may require an internet connection and sends only a hardware fingerprint hash — no personal data. Local user data stays on your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

🎮 Game Development

Games may collect a player ID, game progress, scores, and in-game purchases. Multiplayer games transmit gameplay events to our servers for match synchronisation. We do not display targeted advertising in games we develop unless explicitly agreed with the client and disclosed within the game. Parental consent is obtained for games rated for children.

🔧 IT Consulting Services

During consulting engagements, we may access client systems, codebases, or databases under a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Client data viewed during audits or architecture reviews is never copied, retained, or disclosed beyond the project scope. All consultants sign data confidentiality agreements.

⚡ Custom Software Solutions

Bespoke software is built to client specifications. Data architecture and privacy controls are designed collaboratively with each client. We follow Privacy by Design principles — building data minimisation and access controls into systems from the ground up. Clients own all data stored within custom systems we build for them.

04 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share personal data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted vendors that process data on our behalf — such as cloud hosting (AWS), payment processors (Razorpay / Stripe), and analytics platforms — under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by court order, government authority, or applicable law. We will notify you where permitted to do so.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide 30 days' notice before such a transfer and give you the opportunity to delete your data.
  • With Your Consent: Any other sharing only with your explicit permission.

Third-party services used by BlueFluteX and their respective privacy policies include:

  • Google Analytics / Firebase — Google Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — AWS Privacy Notice
  • Apple App Store — Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play — Google Privacy Policy

05 Data Storage & Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement industry-standard measures:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive databases are encrypted using AES-256.
  • Access Controls: Personal data is accessible only to authorised team members on a need-to-know basis, using role-based access control (RBAC).
  • Secure Development: Our engineering practices include code reviews, dependency audits, and OWASP security guidelines.
  • Incident Response: In the event of a data breach affecting your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and relevant authorities within 72 hours of discovery.
Note: No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication where available.

Our primary data centres are located in India (AWS Mumbai). Some services may use EU or US regions with appropriate safeguards in place.

06 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website (blueflutex.gt.tc) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies:

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function (e.g. session management). Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site (e.g. Google Analytics). These are anonymised and aggregated.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings such as language or display mode.

Mobile Applications

Mobile apps may use device identifiers (Android Advertising ID, Apple IDFA) for analytics. You can reset or opt out of these identifiers through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete Advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Allow Apps to Request to Track

You can manage cookie preferences through our cookie consent banner or your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect core website functionality.

07 Children's Privacy

BlueFluteX takes children's privacy seriously across all our products.

  • Our general services and website are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU under GDPR).
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without verifiable parental consent.
  • For games or apps rated for children (e.g. apps targeting ages under 13), we apply additional safeguards including: no behavioural advertising, no social sharing features, limited data collection to what is strictly necessary for the app to function, and parental consent flows.
  • If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child without appropriate consent, please contact us immediately at [email protected] and we will delete it promptly.
Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising their children's use of our applications. We recommend enabling parental controls on your child's device.

08 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data for marketing or profiling purposes.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Ask us to pause processing your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, email us at [email protected] with the subject line "Data Rights Request". We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request.

Opt-Out of Marketing

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the "Unsubscribe" link in any email, or by emailing us directly. We will process your request within 5 business days.

09 International Data Transfers

BlueFluteX is headquartered in Pune, India. Our primary data storage is within India (AWS Mumbai region). However, some of our third-party service providers may process data in other countries including the United States and European Union.

When we transfer data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission for EU data transfers.
  • Adherence to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable.
  • Compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023.

By using our services from outside India, you consent to the transfer of your information to India and other countries as described in this policy.

10 Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Contact & Inquiry Data: 2 years from last interaction, or until you request deletion.
  • Client Project Data: Duration of project plus 5 years for legal and audit purposes.
  • Account Data: Duration of account plus 90 days after deletion request (for recovery purposes).
  • Usage & Analytics Data: Aggregated data retained for up to 26 months; raw logs purged after 90 days.
  • Financial Records: 7 years as required by Indian tax law.
  • Legal Hold: Data under active legal proceedings may be retained longer.

When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons.

  • We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
  • For significant changes, we will notify you via email (if we have your address) or a prominent notice on our website at least 14 days before the changes take effect.
  • Your continued use of our services after the effective date constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
  • If you do not agree with the changes, you may discontinue use of our services and request data deletion.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
