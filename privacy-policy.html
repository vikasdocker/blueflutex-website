Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version

We use Google Analytics to measure how this site is used. That means Google, not us, receives information about your visit, including your IP address. We run no advertising, no remarketing and no cross-site tracking.

If you submit the contact form we store what you typed so we can reply to you. That is the rest of it.

Analytics

This site loads Google Tag Manager, which is configured to send page views and a small number of interaction events to Google Analytics 4. Tag Manager is the delivery mechanism; Google Analytics is what records the data.

What that means in practice

  • Google receives your IP address as part of serving the request. It uses that to determine an approximate location (country and city level) and does not store the full address.
  • It records the pages you view, the site that referred you, your approximate location, and coarse device and browser details.
  • It sets cookies in your browser. The main ones are _ga and _ga_GTM-NSTRQKNK (a persistent identifier, 2 years by default), _gid (a session identifier, 24 hours) and _gcl_aw (set when a Google tag is linked to an advert, 3 months). Google publishes the authoritative list and current durations in its own documentation.
  • Analytics data is retained for 14 months, the default in our Google Analytics property.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No advertising or remarketing tags are running.
  • No Google Signals, which would let Google enrich analytics with data from your signed-in accounts.
  • No cross-site tracking pixels, and no data sharing with other sites.
  • No third-party advertising cookies.

Opting out

You can prevent this site from measuring your visit by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser extension, or by enabling your browser’s Do Not Track setting. You can also email us at vikasshu7@gmail.com and ask us to stop measuring, or to tell us which reports we should no longer run.

Consent

We are a small studio operating from India and this site is not directed at visitors in the European Economic Area, so we do not present a cookie-consent banner and analytics starts as soon as the page loads.

If you are visiting from the EEA or the UK and you expected a consent choice, please tell us and we will add one. We would rather you had the choice than assumed you did not need it.

What the contact form stores

Only if you submit the form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The budget range you select, if you select one
  • Your message
  • A salted hash of your IP address — used only to rate-limit spam, never to identify you
  • Your browser user agent and the page you submitted from

We do not store your raw IP address, and the hash is salted with a value held in our server configuration, so it cannot be reversed or matched against another site’s data. It is deleted after 24 hours.

Separately, Google Analytics records that a form submission happened and roughly where it came from. It does not receive the contents of your message.

Why we collect it

The form details exist solely to answer your enquiry. Analytics exist so we know which pages are useful and which are not. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell, rent or share either set of data with anyone else. If you would like your details deleted, email us and we will remove them.

Fonts are served from our own server

The typefaces on this site are hosted here, not requested from Google. Had we used a third-party font service, every page view would have sent a font request to Google before anything else on the page had loaded. Self-hosting means our analytics measurement is the only thing that reaches them — and it happens once, at the end.

Other third-party code

The 3D animation in the hero loads three.js from jsDelivr. If your browser downloads it, jsDelivr necessarily sees your IP address, as any content delivery network must. No data is shared with them beyond the ordinary mechanics of serving a file.

If you would rather we did not depend on an external network at all, say so and we will self-host the library instead.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to run the conversation and meet any legal obligation. Rate-limiting records are deleted after 24 hours. Analytics data is retained for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email vikasshu7@gmail.com and we will action it.

Analytics data sits with Google rather than with us, so a request about it is best made directly to them — the Google Analytics data controls page explains the options, including downloading or deleting the data associated with your activity.

This site is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Hosting

The site is hosted on free shared infrastructure operated by InfinityFree. As with any web host, that provider necessarily processes the technical information required to deliver the page.
